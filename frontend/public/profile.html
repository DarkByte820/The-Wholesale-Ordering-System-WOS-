Help & Support

How do I return an item?

You can return items within 30 days of delivery. Visit the order details page and click "Return Item".

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), Credit/Debit Cards, Bank Transfers, and Cash on Delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within 2-5 business days. Express delivery is available for Greater Accra.
